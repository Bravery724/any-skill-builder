# Project Notes Brief

Turn messy project or meeting notes into a readable catch-up brief for colleagues who were absent.

Supply the notes as text or an accessible attachment. You may also provide the audience, meeting date, background, desired language, or length. Unknown owners, dates, and approvals stay unknown.

Start with:

> Use project-notes-brief to turn these notes into a brief for colleagues who missed the meeting: [paste notes]

You receive a concise overview, decisions with recorded reasons, next steps with owners and timing where known, and visible open points. Suggested confirmation steps are labelled separately from meeting commitments.

You can ask: "Group this by topic", "Use recap-only with no new suggestions", "Add source anchors for every material claim", or "Revise the brief using this corrected note: ...". A clear request starts immediately; an empty invocation asks for notes.

No account, network service, or package installation is needed to process pasted text. Reading attached files depends on the host's tools. The skill cannot verify absent facts, transcribe recordings by itself, assign tasks, or send the brief. No automatic trigger or picker behavior is promised.
